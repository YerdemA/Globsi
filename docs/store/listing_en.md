**Full description**

```
Explore the world in the palm of your hand! With Globsi you learn where countries are on a spinning globe, get to know their flags and test yourself with quick quizzes.

🌍 EXPLORE
• Spin the globe with a finger, pinch to zoom.
• Tap a country to see its flag, capital and continent.
• Even the smallest countries, like Vatican City and Monaco, can be picked when you zoom in.

🚩 LEARN FLAGS
• The flags of 202 countries, grouped by continent.
• Flashcards: see the flag, flip the card, learn the answer.
• "Show on globe" takes you from a flag to the map in one tap.

🗺️ MAP PRACTICE
• Find the highlighted country among four choices.

🏳️ FLAG PRACTICE
• Name the country a flag belongs to.

⚙️ MAKE IT YOURS
• Pick one or more continents and a difficulty (easy, medium, hard, mixed).
• With "similar choices" on, wrong answers come from look-alike flags and neighbouring countries — a real challenge!
• Every round has 10 questions, followed by your score and a list of your mistakes.

📊 TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Your accuracy, most confused countries and best scores are kept on your device.

✈️ WORKS OFFLINE
Maps and flags are built in, so you can learn on a plane, on the road, anywhere. No account needed, and your data never leaves your device.

Includes the 193 UN member states, Vatican City and Palestine, as well as partially recognised countries such as Kosovo, Taiwan, Northern Cyprus, Western Sahara, Somaliland, Abkhazia and South Ossetia.

Globsi is free and contains ads. No ads are shown on question screens or while you use the globe.

Map data: Natural Earth. Flags: flag-icons (MIT) and Wikimedia Commons.
```
