# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Globsi is an offline geography learning app developed by Yusuf Erdem Aykaç. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the app.

## Data the app stores

Globsi does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name or email address.

- Your practice statistics (correct and wrong answers per country, best scores, completed rounds), your language and theme choices and the counters that limit how often ads appear (number of sessions, time of the last full-screen ad) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to a server.
- You can delete your statistics at any time in Settings > Reset statistics. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
- Map and flag data are bundled with the app; no internet connection is used for them.

## Advertising

Globsi shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. An internet connection is used to show ads.

- AdMob may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address and ad interactions, in order to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent abuse.
- How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent for ads through Google's consent tool. You can change your choice in Settings > Privacy options.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
- Ad content is limited to a general audience (PG) rating.

## Children

Globsi is not designed specifically for children. The app itself collects no personal data and is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
